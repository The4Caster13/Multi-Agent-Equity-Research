About us

We build the quiet infrastructure behind private markets.

Equity Labs started with a spreadsheet that nobody trusted. Four tabs, thirty years of formulas, and a footnote explaining why the totals were off by six thousand shares. We rebuilt it as a system of record — one place where ownership, terms, and outcomes agree with each other.

Today the team is a mix of fund controllers, transaction lawyers, and engineers. We ship for the people who sign off on the numbers, not the people who present them. That distinction shapes every decision on the roadmap.

What we hold to

Auditable by default

If a number cannot be traced to a document, it does not ship. Every figure in the product carries a path back to the clause that produced it.

Boring on purpose

Finance infrastructure should be predictable. We optimize for no surprises — no silent migrations, no recalculated history, no clever defaults.

Built with controllers

Every release is reviewed by the people who close the books. If it does not survive a quarter-end, it does not leave the branch.

The team

Small on purpose. Every person here has closed a round, audited one, or written the software that failed to.
