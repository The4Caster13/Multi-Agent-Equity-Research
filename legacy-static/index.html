How it works

Cap table math, settled before the round closes.

Three steps from a signed term sheet to a distribution every stakeholder can audit.

See the model → Scenario modeling
01

Connect the documents

Term sheets, SAFEs, and option grants come in as-is. We parse the terms that move the waterfall — liquidation preference, participation, seniority, conversion.

02

Reconcile ownership

Every share class is checked against the source document, and conflicts surface before they compound. Nothing is carried forward on trust.

03

Model and distribute

Run exit scenarios, then publish a statement each holder can open and verify themselves — down to the clause it came from.

Inside the model

Every waterfall is versioned, diffable, and traceable back to the document it came from. Change one term and the distribution recalculates in front of you — with the prior version still on record.
